# Title - the cheese factory game

## Repository
https://github.com/cheesepuffsxd/Cheesefactory

## Description
what I want to do is make a game that is about making cheese. 
It will teach somewhat of a basic process of it and I think its relevent because It will use many of the things we were taught in class.
Also I want to make it because I like cheese :p
I kinda want to make it play like a clicker game because It doesnt sound to difficult but isnt exactly what we were taught in class on how to make.

## Features
- Feature 1 - storage of mulitple materials with a tracker for them
 - done by creating seperate variables and a way to display them on the screen that tracks and updates every tick or smtn
- Feature 2 - mouse with clicker feature to gain materials when clicking on certain areas
 - mouse is able to be detected and will give resources when clicking on a certain area of screen with corresponding material increase in value
- Feature N  - upgrades that can do many of the manual tasks automatically and give bonuses
 - implement upgrades that are purchasable by cheese that will increase material generation

## Challenges
- how to implement an upgrade system/ basically an economy in vs code 
- how to make cheese itself, or at least a cheese that is simple so it fits with this game theme.
- how to make a button or area mouse can click on for resources and not the whole screen.
- making a system so when you click a box on the screen it takes a resource and gives you an output after X amount of time.

## Outcomes
Ideal Outcome:
The ideal outcome is to make a game that has maybe about 4 different resources (cow, milk, curds, cheese )
and have players need to click # amount of times on the cow for milk and then # amount of milk gets turned into curds
then # amount of curds gets turned into cheese after clicking a box and waiting a minute or some amount of time
Then the cheese can be spent on upgrades to increase the amount of milk you get or decrease the amount of times it takes to turn curds into cheese.

Minimal Viable Outcome:
The base essential to make the game is making a button that gives you milk then a sort of fermenting timer that turns milk into cheese and a tracker for how much of it you have.
